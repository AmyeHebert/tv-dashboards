How the grade is built

Scoring

Five categories, each scored 0–100, averaged. A is 90+, B is 80, C is 70, D is 60, below that an F.

Revenue vs goal — October revenue as a share of the annual goal divided by twelve. Capped at 100.
Margin vs target — October gross margin against the unit's own budget margin.
Covered overhead — October gross profit against own operating expense plus the $80,000 fee.
Momentum — October revenue against the unit's own ten-month average. Flat scores 50.
Cost discipline — own operating expense against its own average. At or below average scores 100; every point over costs two.

B50 Products & Parts has no monthly revenue goal and no fee, so it is graded on the three categories that apply. PO sent to NXA is not included — it cannot be measured from these financials.
