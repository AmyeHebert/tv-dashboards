What went well

  • $1,693,545 revenue — third-best month of the year and over the $708,333 monthly goal.
  • $316,505 after overhead, covering its burden for the 7th time in 10 months — the only unit to cover in October.
  • Gross margin recovered to 28.0% from 17.6% in September.
  • $349,123 of new work booked, after two months of net reductions (August and September).

What did not

  • Margin still 7 points under the 35% target, and under target in 7 of 10 months.
  • Backlog down 4 straight months, from $24.24M (June) to $18.60M.
  • Own overhead $77,866, 12% over its own average.
  • Subcontract is 56% of October job cost — margin is riding on sub pricing.
